"Yearning, a WorldTree Orchestral Medley" has been released

published 6 Jun 2026 • tbmassoc Press Office



following the commencement of vtNet, a tbmassoc network; thebelovedmoon and associates (tbmassoc) is proud to debut its accompanying soundtrack -- which also doubles as its musical identity!


but in order to recognize its importance, let's trace back its roots:



세계수 키우기 (The Celestial Tree)



released in 2016 by MadBeat / Mind Blossom, 세계수 키우기 (The Celestial Tree) is a cozy idle-clicker game where your main objective is to grow your own tree and ward off any nightmares that try to siphon off its precious energies!


our benefactor -- Aga-chuu (famously known by her online moniker/username thebelovedmoon) -- has been quite fond of the game when she first got it between Apr-May 2019. more notably, she chose the VIP version (which released 2 years after the original), which was on sale at the time, so she inadvertently got it for free!


her enjoyment of the game has become immense as time goes by; however, her greatest slump -- noted as the Jun 2019 restart -- has forced her to let go of her precious game, alongside everything else at the time. but her memories haven't faded yet -- she passed the game along to her close friend, Emi (Emmannuel Ortega). he has since taken care of the tree that was bestowed from her and made it worthwhile.
