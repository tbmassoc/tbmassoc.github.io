vtNet commences Jun 2026

published 23 Mar 2026 • tbmassoc Press Office



La Luna 🌙 is hereby proud to present the newest addition to its ever-growing creative suite: vtNet -- formerly vTbmassoc; a collection of vtuber channels created and maintained by Aga-chuu (@thebelovedmoon), the benefactor of the Luna 🌙 and the First Maria🍃 of VTresMarias!!


this collection is born from the idea that any vtuber can express themselves in multitudes of ways, across ecosystems.


in Aga's case, that expansion of ecosystems began with the commencement of Emii Shion (now known as 絵美紫苑とアーサー王) -- adjoined together through a mutual acquisition following the Third Maria🌸's exit from AURORA. it then expanded further following her success in other ventures; including Sakura Sumiyoi and GourmetDayLife, where the latter became the home of Senri Mana (千里真那Ch).


following the continuous expansion of ideas and ecosystems across fandoms -- the most recent being Detroit Become Human's RT600i, vtNet is consolidating all of it to a single platform where all experiences meet; marking a central point for all things vtubing.
